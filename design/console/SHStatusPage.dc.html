Used for links in the console, emails, the feed and badges. pulse does not manage DNS or certificates.

Point your reverse proxy at the pulse-status container and let the proxy handle TLS. For example:

# Caddyfile status.quillbird.example { reverse_proxy pulse-status:8080 }

pulse-status sends ETag and Cache-Control on the API, the feed and badges, so the proxy or a CDN may cache them and answer repeat visits with 304. Purge that cache after you make the page more private.
