Why this step is required

The owner and every admin must have a second factor. In v1 this is an authenticator app (TOTP). Members and viewers may add one; you can require it for everyone later in Settings.

The key is stored encrypted with the key file from PULSE_CONSOLE_SECRET_KEY_FILE.
