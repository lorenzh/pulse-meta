Account security

How you sign in to pulse as mara@quillbird.example: a password and an authenticator app.

Two-factor authentication

On since 2026-10-30 required for owner and admins

Two-factor is required for the owner and every admin. Members and viewers may add it, and must keep it when the owner requires it for everyone.
