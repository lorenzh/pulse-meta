Questions

Can I move from self-hosted to hosted, and back?

Yes. Hosted pulse runs the same open-source code and reads the same checks.json. Upload your file to start; export it with your history to leave.

Can I monitor services inside my network?

Run a pulse-probe inside your network with a token from your hosted account. It only sends results out; nothing needs to reach in.

Who counts as an open-source project?

A project whose source is public under an OSI-approved licence and that you monitor for its users, such as a docs site, a package registry or a demo. Apply with the repository link; we review every request and renew it once a year.

Where is my data stored?

In Frankfurt, Germany, with encrypted backups in Helsinki, Finland; both stay in the EU. Results are kept for 90 days on Starter and one year on Team, incidents for as long as your account exists.

Who watches the watcher?

Hosted pulse has its own public status page at status.losoft.dev, monitored from a separate provider.
